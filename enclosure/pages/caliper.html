Piano caliper guide

The sizes needed to decide where the ESP32, the amp and the wires go inside the Chicco toy. Started 2026-10-17. All values in millimetres.

How to measure

  • Always edge to edge. Nothing is measured from the centre of a hole.
  • Depth into a shell: lay a ruler on its edge across the shell, resting on both rims. Then push the caliper's depth rod (the thin rod that comes out of the end) down from the ruler to the surface. A ruler held upright works too.
  • Shells lie open, inside up, as on the table now.
  • The numbers in grey are what I'd assume without you. Yours replace them.

Room above the main board when the case is closed = step 1 + step 2.
