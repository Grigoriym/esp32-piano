# Sounds

`meow.wav`: 3.78-4.85 s of [Meow of a pleading cat](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_pleading_cat.oga)
by Heismark, public domain on Wikimedia Commons. Mono, 44.1 kHz, otherwise unchanged.

`siamese.wav`: 0.30-1.06 s of [Meow of a Siamese cat](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_Siamese_cat_-_freemaster2.wav)
by freemaster2, CC0 on Wikimedia Commons. 44.1 kHz, otherwise unchanged. The source
peaks at full scale, about 40 samples of it clipped.

`tools/gen_cat_sf2.py` turns them into the cat instruments.
