Random visualizer for the 32×8 matrix

Every scene reacts to the same made-up song: the beat at 124 bpm and a 2-bar loop with layers added one by one (bass, chords, hook), then live keys. Keys A S D F G H J K L on your keyboard play live notes too. Red, on or off per dot, one brightness for the whole matrix, like the real MAX7219. Each card re-rolls its random settings every 4 bars. Click the scenes you want in the rotation.

The rotation: a random scene every 4 bars, on the bar line
