Chicco Piano

What every button and knob does since the ESP32 took over.

Buttons

Control

RESETBack to normal: piano sound, full volume, normal octave, every note off, demo and beat stopped, loop erased
VOLUME +Louder, one step per press
VOLUME −Quieter, one step per press (a few presses and it's silent)
TEMPO +Keys one octave up (up to two)
TEMPO −Keys one octave down (down to two)
DEMOPlays the Megalovania opening twice; press again to stop
RECORDRecords a loop from your first note (keys and drums); press again and it repeats, keeping its sound while you switch style and play over it. With the beat on it starts on the bar line and its length rounds to whole bars. Press while it plays to add a layer on top (up to 4), again on a loop start to keep it: you hear it from then on. Played over two passes (say, a part, then a pass of silence), the loop becomes two passes long and the older layers repeat in it. Press while stopped to start a new loop
REPLAYStops the loop, or starts it again from the top (on the next bar line with the beat on)
BLUE BIRDUndo: drops the layer being added, or the last layer; the last one left erases the loop
Knob turnNext screen: Status, Play, Groove, Layers, Debug. On Layers it first walks down the layers, one per click
Knob pressOn the Layers screen: mutes the highlighted layer, or brings it back. With the beat or metronome on it happens on the next bar line ("mute at bar"); the layer stays in the loop. Muted layers are dim on the LED strip
SELECTStarts the drum beat of the last style button (BLUES if none yet); press again to stop. A style button while it plays changes only the keys' sound: the beat (and a loop on it) keeps going. For another beat: SELECT off, the style button, SELECT on. Not recorded by the loop

Style buttons pick the sound and the beat

SLOW ROCKEDM mode too (saw lead, keys in A minor), with a techno beat: kick on every beat, clap on 2 and 4, 16th hats, open hat between the beats, 130 bpm
ROCKDubstep, EDM mode too (keys in A minor): every key plays a crushed, heavy growl bass; beat: half-time dubstep, one big snare a bar, 140 bpm
NEW AGEA second cat, a deeper Siamese meow, in tune with the key; beat: soft ride and rim click, 80 bpm
DISCOEDM mode: saw lead, keys play only A minor (key 1 = A1, 7 keys an octave); beat: EDM, 124 bpm
MARCHEDM mode too (saw lead, keys in A minor), with a breakbeat: kick on 1 and between the beats, clap on 2 and 4, 124 bpm
WALTZBalkan: accordion; beat: čoček in 2/4, 126 bpm
SAMBAMarimba; beat: samba with shaker and agogo, 100 bpm
BLUESElectric piano; beat: blues shuffle, 88 bpm

DOGBass drum

Knobs, in three sections

Volume

BeatThe beat's loudness; all the way down drops it out
KeysThe keys you play live; held notes follow it. A loop layer keeps the level it was recorded at

Metronome

Tempo60-180 bpm (with a beat playing it follows the beat)
VolumeThe click's loudness (FROG turns it on and off)

Effects

FilterLeft dark, right bright; live keys and DEMO only, a loop layer keeps its own
ResonanceLeft none, right a whistling peak at the filter's edge; turn the filter with it. Gets quieter as it rises
ReverbLeft dry, middle the sound's own, right a huge room; live keys and DEMO only
EchoHow much chick 1's Echo repeats: left 1 quiet repeat, right 8 long ones
ReleaseHow long a note rings after you let go: left short, middle the sound's own, right ~2 s
TremoloLeft none, right the volume pulses fast, at the top it chops

Chicks are effects

Laid out like on the toy: 1-4 the top row, 5-8 the bottom row, from the left. The screen shows them in that order (E H O A, R T S W), lit while on. A loop records Echo, Hold, Octave, Arp and Stutter with your notes; Wide, Rise and Tape are only heard live. RESET turns them all off.

1EchoOn / off: each note comes back, softer, a dotted 8th apart; how many and how soft: the Echo knob
2HoldOn / off: notes ring after you let go
3OctaveOn / off: each key plays an octave higher too
4ArpOn / off: held keys play one at a time, low to high, on every 16th
5RiseFor one bar the keys climb an octave and the filter opens, then back
6Tape stopFor half a bar keys and loop slide an octave down, then the keys stop
7StutterWhile held: the keys you hold repeat on every 16th
8WideOn / off: two slightly detuned copies of each note, a thicker sound
