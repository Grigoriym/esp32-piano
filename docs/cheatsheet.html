Megalovania opening

Four bars. Only the first two notes change from bar to bar (keys 10, 8, 7, 6); the rest of each bar is the same run. One box is one 16th; a wider box is held longer. Press DEMO on the toy to hear it at 100 bpm; Listen here uses the metronome's tempo below.

Metronome

Four clicks per bar, the first one higher. Start slow and step up: the original runs at 120, the toy's DEMO at 100. Listen here plays at the tempo set here; with the metronome running it comes in on the next beat 1, and a tempo change speeds up or slows down both at once.

Ej, od kad sam se rodio (Sinan Sakić)

The chords, in A minor, as found on chord sites (pesmarica.rs, akordi-online.com). The tune itself isn't written here, only the chords to play under it or to sing over. Press WALTZ for the Balkan sound (accordion), then SELECT for its čoček beat, and change chord every bar (two beats).

Why these hand positions: the keys are wired as a grid and only some have diodes, so some three-key chords (12 + 15 + 20, for one) can make a fourth key look pressed, and the toy then holds notes back. These avoid that.

Buttons

Control

RESETBack to normal: piano sound, full volume, normal octave, every note off, demo and beat stopped, loop erased
VOLUME +Louder, one step per press
VOLUME −Quieter, one step per press (a few presses and it's silent)
TEMPO +Keys one octave up (up to two)
TEMPO −Keys one octave down (down to two)
DEMOPlays the Megalovania opening twice; press again to stop
RECORDRecords a loop from your first note (keys and drums); press again and it repeats right away, keeping its sound while you switch style and play over it. Press while it plays to record a new one
REPLAYStops the loop, or starts it again from the top
SELECTStarts the drum beat of the last style button (ROCK if none yet); press again to stop. A style button while it plays switches the beat at the next bar. Not recorded by the loop
