Chicco Piano

What every button and knob does since the ESP32 took over.

Buttons

Control

RESETBack to normal: piano sound, full volume, normal octave, every note off, demo and beat stopped, loop erased
VOLUME +Louder, one step per press
VOLUME −Quieter, one step per press (a few presses and it's silent)
TEMPO +Keys one octave up (up to two)
TEMPO −Keys one octave down (down to two)
DEMOPlays the Megalovania opening twice; press again to stop
RECORDRecords a loop from your first note (keys and drums); press again and it repeats, keeping its sound while you switch style and play over it. With the beat on it starts on the bar line and its length rounds to whole bars. Press while it plays to add a layer on top (up to 4), again on a loop start to keep it: you hear it from then on. Played over two passes (say, a part, then a pass of silence), the loop becomes two passes long and the older layers repeat in it. Press while stopped to start a new loop
REPLAYStops the loop, or starts it again from the top (on the next bar line with the beat on)
Knob pressUndo: drops the layer being added, or the last layer; the last one left erases the loop
SELECTStarts the drum beat of the last style button (BLUES if none yet); press again to stop. A style button while it plays changes only the keys' sound: the beat (and a loop on it) keeps going. For another beat: SELECT off, the style button, SELECT on. Not recorded by the loop

Style buttons pick the sound and the beat

SLOW ROCKEDM mode too (saw lead, keys in A minor), with a techno beat: kick on every beat, clap on 2 and 4, 16th hats, open hat between the beats, 130 bpm
ROCKDubstep, EDM mode too (keys in A minor): every key plays a crushed, heavy growl bass; beat: half-time dubstep, one big snare a bar, 140 bpm
NEW AGEA second cat, a deeper Siamese meow, in tune with the key; beat: soft ride and rim click, 80 bpm
DISCOEDM mode: saw lead, keys play only A minor (key 1 = A1, 7 keys an octave); beat: EDM, 124 bpm
MARCHEDM mode too (saw lead, keys in A minor), with a breakbeat: kick on 1 and between the beats, clap on 2 and 4, 124 bpm
WALTZBalkan: accordion; beat: čoček in 2/4, 126 bpm
SAMBAMarimba; beat: samba with shaker and agogo, 100 bpm
BLUESElectric piano; beat: blues shuffle, 88 bpm

Animals play drums

DOGBass drum
FROGMetronome on / off (its knobs: the Metronome section)
RED BIRDHigh-mid tom
BLUE BIRDOpen hi-hat

Knobs, in three sections

Volume: beatThe beat's loudness; all the way down drops it out
Volume: keysThe keys you play live; held notes follow it. A loop layer keeps the level it was recorded at
Effects: filterLeft dark, right bright; live keys and DEMO only, a loop layer keeps its own
Effects: reverbLeft dry, middle the sound's own, right a huge room; live keys and DEMO only
Metronome: tempo60-180 bpm (with a beat playing it follows the beat)
Metronome: volumeThe click's loudness (FROG turns it on and off)

Chicks are effects

Two rows of four, counted from the top left: 1-4 the top row, 5-8 the bottom row. The screen shows them in that order (E H O A, R T S W), lit while on. A loop records Echo, Hold, Octave, Arp and Stutter with your notes; Wide, Rise and Tape are only heard live. RESET turns them all off.

Chick 1Echo on / off: each note comes back 3 times, softer, a dotted 8th apart
Chick 2Hold on / off: notes ring after you let go
Chick 3Octave on / off: each key plays an octave higher too
Chick 4Arp on / off: held keys play one at a time, low to high, on every 16th
Chick 5Rise: for one bar the keys climb an octave and the filter opens, then back
Chick 6Tape stop: for half a bar keys and loop slide an octave down, then the keys stop
Chick 7Stutter, while held: the keys you hold repeat on every 16th
Chick 8Wide on / off: two slightly detuned copies of each note, a thicker sound
