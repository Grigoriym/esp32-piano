Chicco Riffs

Things to play over the EDM beat. Listen first (clips: tools/render_riffs.py after a change), then try it on the toy, then tell Claude what to change.

On the toy

  1. Start Chicco Piano from the app menu.
  2. Press DISCO (EDM mode: every key is in A minor, so nothing sounds wrong), then SELECT for the beat.
  3. Play the numbers below. Keys 1, 8, 15, 22, 29 are all A: each run of 7 keys is one octave.
  4. Chords are always a key, skip one, a key, skip one, a key.

Stacking layers

  1. Beat running: RECORD, play the bass, RECORD again around the end. It snaps to whole bars, so close is good enough.
  2. RECORD again while it plays: play the chords on top, RECORD to keep them. Then the hook the same way (up to 4 layers).
  3. Didn't like the last one: press the knob to undo it. REPLAY stops and restarts the loop (on the next bar).
